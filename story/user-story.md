## Outcome, scope and context

As an authorized business buyer, I want to see my order's shipment carrier, tracking link, status and available delivery dates so I can follow delivery. For this POC there is no ERP: clearly labeled test shipments already in Salesforce are the source. The middleware should read them, obtain USPS tracking through an authorized carrier API, and update the corresponding Salesforce records. UPS and FedEx use simulated responses until suitable test access is available. Carrier polling at 07:15 and 19:15 Eastern Time remains the proposed schedule; the ERP dispatch job is deferred. No inbound public webhook is needed. FulfillmentOrder and ShipmentItem creation are deferred, subject to proving the required behavior is possible. Each project supplies environment configuration and credentials outside Git.
